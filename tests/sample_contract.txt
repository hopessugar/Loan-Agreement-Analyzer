LOAN AGREEMENT

This Loan Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 1st January 2026 between ABC Microfinance Limited, a company registered under the Companies Act 2013, having its registered office at 12, Gandhi Nagar, Jaipur, Rajasthan ("Lender") and Ramesh Kumar Sharma, son of Mohan Lal Sharma, residing at Village Badgaon, Tehsil Girwa, District Udaipur, Rajasthan 313001 ("Borrower").

CLAUSE 1 - LOAN AMOUNT AND DISBURSEMENT

The Lender agrees to disburse a loan of Rs. 50,000 (Rupees Fifty Thousand Only) to the Borrower on the date of execution of this Agreement. The loan amount shall be credited directly to the Borrower's bank account number ending in 4821 held at State Bank of India, Udaipur branch.

CLAUSE 2 - INTEREST RATE

The Borrower shall pay interest at the rate of 24% per annum on a flat rate basis on the outstanding principal amount. Interest shall be calculated on the original loan amount for the entire tenure of the loan regardless of repayments made.

CLAUSE 3 - REPAYMENT SCHEDULE

The Borrower shall repay the loan in 24 equal monthly instalments of Rs. 3,833 each. The first instalment shall be due on 1st February 2026 and subsequent instalments on the 1st of each month thereafter. The total repayment amount shall be Rs. 96,000.

CLAUSE 4 - PROCESSING FEE AND ADMINISTRATIVE CHARGES

A processing fee of Rs. 2,500 shall be deducted at source from the disbursed loan amount. Additionally, an administrative handling charge of Rs. 800 shall be collected at the time of disbursement. These charges are non-refundable under any circumstances.

CLAUSE 5 - INSURANCE

The Borrower is required to purchase a credit life insurance policy at a premium of Rs. 1,200 per annum. The insurance premium shall be collected upfront for the full loan tenure, amounting to Rs. 2,400 for the 24-month period. Failure to maintain insurance coverage shall be treated as a default event.

CLAUSE 6 - LATE PAYMENT

In the event of delay in payment of any instalment beyond the due date, the Borrower shall be liable to pay a late payment fee of Rs. 250 per day for each day of delay. This charge shall be applied from the first day following the due date without any grace period.

CLAUSE 7 - PENALTY INTEREST

In addition to the late payment fee, the Lender reserves the right to charge penalty interest at 36% per annum on the overdue amount for the period of default. Penalty interest shall be compounded monthly on unpaid balances.

CLAUSE 8 - PREPAYMENT

The Borrower may prepay the loan in full or in part at any time. In the event of prepayment, the Borrower shall pay a prepayment penalty of 5% of the outstanding principal amount at the time of prepayment. Partial prepayments shall not reduce the monthly instalment amount or the loan tenure.

CLAUSE 9 - DEFAULT AND RECOVERY

In the event of three consecutive missed instalments, the entire outstanding loan amount along with all accrued interest, fees, and charges shall become immediately due and payable. The Lender reserves the right to initiate recovery proceedings including legal action and reporting to credit bureaus.

CLAUSE 10 - GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Udaipur, Rajasthan.
